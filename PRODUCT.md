# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers, founders, product leaders and recruiters hiring a senior or AI-native product manager.
They scan for proof in under a minute, then open one or two case studies.
Secondary: PMs and builders who read the notes.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Abhibhaw Asthana, a product manager who builds.
It shows the problems he found, what he decided to build and leave out, and the measured results.
Success: a hiring manager emails him or downloads the resume.

## Positioning

A domain-agnostic, AI-native PM with builder instinct, not an infra or dev-tool PM.
One pattern runs through the work: find where people wait, remove the wait, and keep it safe.
He builds first versions himself with AI coding agents.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, a resume link or a referral, on a laptop or a phone.
The one-page resume PDF is generated from the same source as /resume/.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hugo static site with a custom theme, deployed to Cloudflare Workers on push to main.
- Content lives in Markdown front matter: work cases, in-development projects, notes, lab.
- Only public Devtron material may appear. The Devtron AI Control Plane is unannounced: no product name, no screenshots.
- Two unannounced side projects are never named on the site.

## Brand Commitments

- Writing follows ASD-STE100-style plain English. No em dashes.
- The "correction mark": an old wait struck through in red, the new figure beside it. Red means "removed" only.
- Work is grouped by organization: Devtron, BharatPe, independent.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real metrics, approved by the owner: GPU tickets -80%+, utilization +23%, Atlas root cause hours to ~1 min, BharatPe 14 days to 17 min and 20,000+ requests, Devtron trial 4x leads and 33% faster conversion.
- Public Devtron product screenshots in hugo/static/images/work/.
- Portrait: the GitHub avatar (https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/39991296), approved for use.
- Absent, never to be fabricated: testimonials, customer logos, pilot results or users for Sakhi, Dhanvantari or Peira, and stand-in photographs of people.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before adjectives: every claim on the page has a number, a decision or a public source.
2. Show what was left out, not only what was built.
3. In-development projects show decisions and status, never results.
4. A hiring manager gets the point from the first screen.
